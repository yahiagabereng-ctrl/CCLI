CEI 0-16 · Central Plant Controller

CCI Compliance FAQ

Fifteen questions and answers on the CCI (Controllore Centrale di Impianto), sourced from Higeco More's public FAQ for the Senergy CCI product — higecomore.com.

Obligation & scope Measurement & data Control & integration Certification & security
Q01Obligation
For which systems is the CCI mandatory?
Per Resolution 385/25/R/eel and 564/25/R/eel: photovoltaic and wind plants with nominal power ≥ 100 kW, always connected at medium voltage (MT).
Q02Obligation
For which power plant technologies is CCI mandatory?
For plants ≥ 1 MW on medium voltage, the obligation applies to all technologies, without distinction. Below 1 MW on MV, only photovoltaic and wind require a CCI.
Q03Obligation
For which power plants is the bonus foreseen?
Only for existing plants under 1 MW: financial contributions from €3,000 to €10,000, depending on submission timing and plant size.
Q04Obligation
What functions must the CCI have? Are they all mandatory?
Per CEI 0-16 Annexes O and T, the CCI must support functional performances PF1, PF2, and PF3 — but which are mandatory to actually activate varies by the specific regulation in force.
Q05Control
Is NTP time synchronization compliant with Annex O?
No. Time sync must come either from a GPS receiver integrated into the CCI, or via NTS — the secure, authenticated version of NTP.
Q06Certification
Does the CCI need to be connected to the internet?
"It better be." The CCI's PKI management benefits from cloud-based certificate lifecycle management and remote updates.
Q07Measurement
What data must be collected by the CCI?
At the delivery point: P, Q, V (and optionally I), plus device status. Per generator: power measurements and status above specified thresholds.
Q08Measurement
How should P, Q and V measurements be made at the delivery point?
MV measurements must be taken directly by the CCI's own measurement unit, preferably with Class 0.2 (or better) converters and Class 0.5 (or better) CT/VT.
Q09Measurement
Can I read voltage from the PI and current from the PG?
No. Protective-relay CTs (used by the PI/PG protection devices) cannot guarantee the accuracy required for CCI measurement.
Q10Measurement
Can I install Rogowski probes instead of measuring CTs?
Yes, but they are less precise than CTs — CEI 0-16 requires total measurement error on P and Q to stay below 2.2%.
Q11Control
How does the CCI control the plant?
Via closed-loop regulators that read the delivery-point measurements and compare them against set points — either received (servo mode) or calculated (autonomous mode).
Q12Control
Can the CCI communicate with inverters via an existing monitoring system?
Yes, provided the connection still meets the standard's requirements — including ≥ 99% annual availability and the specified response times.
Q13Certification
Who must perform the CCI certification?
Manufacturers may issue a self-declaration of conformity after testing. A third-party accredited body can additionally certify against these annexes.
Q14Certification
What cybersecurity certifications are required?
IEC 62443-4-1 (IsaSecure SDLA) and IEC 62443-4-2 (IsaSecure CSA), plus cryptographic components certified to FIPS 140-2, level 3.
Q15Certification
Can CCI compliance tests be carried out in any laboratory?
No. Insulation, EMC, noise immunity and climate tests need an ISO 17025 accredited lab; functional tests need an ISO 17065 accredited inspection body.
