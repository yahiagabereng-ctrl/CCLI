# P5-G02 GOOSE wire evidence — 2026-10-05

## Root cause (fixed)

GOOSE was configured on `br-lan`, but TesproOS TR544 has **empty br-lan** (no bridge members).
Plant port is **lan3** (`lan2_sec`, 192.168.30.1/24).

Fix: `goose.interface: lan3` in lab_tr400_cleartext_tsp_ttyS1.yaml, deployed to /etc/ccli/lab.yaml, ccli restarted.

Boot log after fix:
  mms: GOOSE publishing enabled on lan3 (gcb_PdC_Mis4sec APPID=0x1000, gcb_Stato_Allarmi APPID=0x1001)
