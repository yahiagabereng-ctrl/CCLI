If decoded as naive big-endian (first reg = high word), P1 ≈ 397 W and V/f are nonsense — matches garbage in ccli logread.

Conclusion:
  Modbus TCP **works** from DUT when ccli not holding the socket.
  Metrology is **plausible** with **word-swapped** float32 (low reg first).
  ccli modbus_adapter uses regs_to_float32_be(regs[p], regs[p+1]) — likely **wrong endian/word order** for EMT432.
